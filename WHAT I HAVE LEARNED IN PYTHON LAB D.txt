1) what is a fork ?

this topic was brought up in class and given in one example which goes as 

import os

a = "hello world"
os.fork()
os.fork()
os.fork()
print(a)


here the hello world would print around 8 times and the reason behind it is 2**3 or (2**n formula) time fork but the main reason is 

• 1st fork(): P1 splits into two. Now you have 2 processes (P1 and C1). Both move on to the second fork().
• 2nd fork(): Both P1 and C1 split into two. Now you have 4 processes (P1, C1, plus two new ones: C2 and C3). All 4 move on to the third fork().
• 3rd fork(): All 4 processes split into two again. Now you have 8 processes running at the exact same time.

7) what is hdf5?
HDF5 is a high-performance file format designed to store massive amounts of complex data in a single file—essentially acting like a complete file system inside one file.

* **Groups:** Act like folders to organize your data.
* **Datasets:** Act like files that hold the actual numbers, tables, or images.
* **Attributes:** Act like sticky notes that save metadata (like units or dates) right next to the data.

It is widely used in science and AI because it lets you compress huge datasets and read tiny pieces of a massive file directly from your hard drive without overloading your computer's RAM.
